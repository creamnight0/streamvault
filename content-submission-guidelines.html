Content Submission Guidelines

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Every upload must meet these requirements before it is published. Submissions are checked by automated systems and human moderators.

1. Eligibility

  • You must hold a StreamVault account in good standing
  • You must be the rights holder or hold written authorization for all content submitted
  • Accounts must have a verified email address to publish

2. Mandatory Certifications at Upload

At the time of each submission you must certify that:

  • Every identifiable person in the video has consented to appear and to distribution on StreamVault
  • The content contains no minors in unsafe, exploitative, or sexualized contexts (see Child Safety Policy)
  • The content does not infringe any third-party rights
  • The content complies with the Content Guidelines

3. Technical Requirements

  • Formats: MP4, MOV, WEBM • max 10 GB • max 12 hours
  • Minimum resolution 480p; videos below 720p are not eligible for monetization
  • No corrupted files, test cards, or static-image “videos” used to game search

4. Review Process

Uploads are scanned automatically (fingerprinting, hash-matching against known illegal content databases, audio ID) and sampled for human review. Most videos publish within minutes; flagged videos may take up to 48 hours. You will be notified of the outcome and can appeal under the Complaints & Appeals Policy.

5. Consequences of Violations

Violations result in removal, strikes, demonetization, or account termination depending on severity. Illegal content is reported to relevant authorities (see Illegal Content Reporting Policy).
