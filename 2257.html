Exemption Statement

StreamVault does not produce, commission, or host sexually explicit content. All videos on this platform are general-audience entertainment, educational, and creative works. Accordingly, StreamVault and the content on this site are not subject to the record-keeping requirements of 18 U.S.C. § 2257.

To the extent any content were ever deemed subject to § 2257, the operators of this website are not the “producers” of such content within the meaning of 28 C.F.R. § 75.1(c), and act solely as a distribution platform.

Uploader Responsibility

Every uploader certifies at the time of submission that their content complies with all applicable laws, our Content Guidelines, and our Content Submission Guidelines. Content that violates these requirements is removed and the account is actioned under our enforcement policies.
