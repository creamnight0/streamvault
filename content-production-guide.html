Content Production Guide

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Best practices for producing content that performs well on StreamVault and sails through review. This guide is advisory; the binding rules live in the Content Guidelines and Content Submission Guidelines.

1. Before You Film

  • Secure written permission from every identifiable person appearing in your video
  • Verify and document that every participant is an adult where any age-restricted rating could apply
  • Confirm you hold rights to all music, footage, artwork, and fonts you plan to use
  • Scout locations and obtain filming permits where required

2. Technical Recommendations

  • Resolution: 1080p minimum; 4K preferred for the 4K badge
  • Frame rate: constant 24/30/60 fps
  • Audio: -14 LUFS loudness target, no clipping, stereo AAC 320 kbps
  • Container: MP4 (H.264/H.265) or WEBM (VP9/AV1)
  • Thumbnails: 1280×720 JPG/PNG, no misleading imagery

3. Metadata That Works

  • Titles: descriptive and honest — clickbait that misrepresents content is removed
  • Descriptions: first 150 characters matter most for search
  • Tags: 5–15 relevant tags; irrelevant tag-stuffing is penalized
  • Category: choose the single best fit; miscategorized videos rank lower

4. Documentation to Keep

Retain releases, licenses, and consent records for as long as your content is live plus seven years. You may be asked to produce them during a review or dispute.

5. Accessibility

Add captions or subtitles where possible; caption uploads in SRT/VTT are supported. Audio descriptions expand your audience and improve discoverability.
