Law Enforcement Requests

Last updated: September 5, 2026

This page describes how law enforcement agencies may request user data or content preservation from StreamVault, and the standards we apply.

1. Where to Send Requests

Official requests should be submitted from a government email domain to lawenforcement@streamvault.example, or by post to: Legal Department, StreamVault Ltd., 123 Example Street, Suite 100, Example City, EX 00000. We do not accept requests via social media or phone.

2. What We Require

  • Basic subscriber information: valid subpoena or equivalent legal process
  • Non-content records (e.g., IP logs): court order or equivalent
  • Content of communications: search warrant or equivalent
  • Cross-border requests: MLAT or letters rogatory unless another lawful mechanism applies

Each request must identify the requesting agency, the responsible officer, the legal authority, and the specific account(s) or URL(s) concerned, and be narrowly tailored.

3. Emergency Disclosure

Where we have a good-faith belief that disclosure is necessary to prevent imminent death or serious physical harm, we may disclose limited data without legal process. Mark such requests “EMERGENCY” and include the facts establishing the emergency.

4. Preservation

Upon a valid preservation request we will preserve identified records for 90 days, renewable once, pending issuance of legal process.

5. User Notice & Transparency

Our policy is to notify users of requests for their data unless prohibited by law or where notice would risk harm. We publish aggregate statistics in a periodic transparency report.
