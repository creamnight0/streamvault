Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information StreamVault collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. Template text for a demo site.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account data — username, email address, date of birth, password (hashed)
  • Content & activity — uploads, comments, likes, watch history, searches
  • Device & technical data — IP address, browser type, device identifiers, approximate location
  • Payment data — handled by our payment processors; we never store full card numbers
  • Age assurance data — where required by law, processed as described in our Age Assurance Policy

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide, personalize, and improve the Service
  • Recommend videos and creators
  • Process payments and creator payouts
  • Enforce our Terms and Content Guidelines, and keep the platform safe
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data with service providers (hosting, payments, analytics), with other users to the extent you make it public (e.g., comments), and with authorities where legally required (see Law Enforcement Requests).

4. Cookies

See our Cookie Notice for details on cookies and similar technologies and how to control them.

5. Data Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes described here or as required by law. You can delete your account at any time from account settings; deletion requests are honored within 30 days, subject to legal retention obligations.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, port, or object to processing of your personal data. Submit requests to privacy@streamvault.example. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

7. International Transfers

Where data is transferred across borders, we use appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

8. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. See our Child Safety Policy.
