Cookie Notice

Last updated: September 5, 2026

This notice explains how StreamVault uses cookies and similar technologies, and how you can control them.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We also use localStorage and similar technologies; this notice covers all of them.

2. Categories We Use

  • Strictly necessary — sign-in state, security, load balancing. Cannot be switched off.
  • Functional — theme preference (dark/light), language, volume/quality settings.
  • Analytics — aggregate usage statistics that help us improve the Service.
  • Advertising — used only for non-Premium users to show and cap ads; never used for under-18 accounts.

3. Your Choices

  • Set your preferences in the cookie banner shown on first visit, or anytime via Settings → Privacy → Cookies
  • Browser controls: block or delete cookies in your browser settings (some features may break)
  • Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals are honored where legally required

4. Retention

Session cookies expire when you close your browser. Persistent cookies last from 30 days (analytics) up to 12 months (preferences). Consent is re-requested at least every 12 months.

Questions: privacy@streamvault.example. See also our Privacy Policy.
