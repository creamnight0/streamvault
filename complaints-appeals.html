Complaints & Appeals Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

We make mistakes. This policy explains how to challenge a moderation decision — whether your content was removed, your account restricted, or a report you filed was rejected.

1. What You Can Appeal

  • Removal or age-restriction of your content
  • Strikes, demonetization, and account suspensions or terminations
  • Rejection of a report or removal request you submitted
  • Age assurance outcomes

2. How to Appeal

Every enforcement notification includes an Appeal link. Alternatively, email appeals@streamvault.example with the video URL or case number and a short explanation. Appeals must be filed within 60 days of the decision.

3. How Appeals Are Handled

  • Appeals are reviewed by a human moderator who was not involved in the original decision
  • We aim to resolve appeals within 7 days; complex cases may take up to 30 days
  • Outcomes: decision upheld, decision reversed (content restored / strike removed), or modified action
  • You will receive a reasoned outcome notification

4. Further Recourse

If you disagree with the appeal outcome, you may have additional options depending on your jurisdiction, such as certified out-of-court dispute settlement bodies (EU DSA) or complaints to your national regulator. Nothing in this policy limits your right to go to court.
