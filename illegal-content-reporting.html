Illegal Content Reporting Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

This policy describes how anyone — users or non-users — can report content they believe is illegal, and how StreamVault handles such reports.

1. What to Report Here

  • Child sexual abuse or exploitation (see also Child Safety Policy)
  • Non-consensual intimate imagery
  • Terrorist or violent extremist content
  • Content depicting or facilitating human trafficking
  • Threats of violence, harassment amounting to a criminal offence
  • Sale of illegal goods or services
  • Fraud and scams

2. How to Report

  • In-product: the 🚩 Report button on every video, comment, and channel
  • Web form: the Content Removal Request form (select “Illegal content”)
  • Email: legal-reports@streamvault.example — include URLs and a description

3. What Happens Next

  • Reports of CSAM and imminent-harm content are triaged 24/7 with priority handling
  • Confirmed illegal content is removed, hashes are added to prevention databases, and accounts are terminated
  • Where legally required or appropriate, we preserve evidence and notify law enforcement
  • Reporters receive an acknowledgment and, where lawful, an outcome notification

4. No Retaliation, No Charge

Reporting is free, available to non-account-holders, and reports made in good faith will never result in action against the reporter. Knowingly false reports may be actioned.
