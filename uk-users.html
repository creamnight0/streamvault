Information for Users in the UK

Last updated: September 5, 2026

This page explains how StreamVault complies with the UK Online Safety Act 2023 (OSA) and what UK users can expect. Template text for a demo site.

1. Our Duties Under the Online Safety Act

As a user-to-user service accessible in the UK, StreamVault takes proportionate measures to protect users from illegal content and to protect children from content that is harmful to them. This includes proactive detection technology, robust reporting tools, and age assurance where required.

2. Age Assurance

Where UK law requires that certain content only be shown to adults, we apply highly effective age assurance as described in our Age Assurance Policy. We do not rely on self-declaration alone for age-restricted content.

3. Reporting and Complaints

UK users can report content through the Report button on every video or via the Content Removal Request form. Complaints about our moderation decisions — including decisions to remove or not remove content, and account restrictions — are handled under our Complaints & Appeals Policy.

4. Terms Clarity

Our Terms of Service and Content Guidelines describe what is and is not allowed on the platform and how our systems enforce those rules.

5. Regulator

The UK regulator for online safety is Ofcom. Information about the Online Safety Act is available at ofcom.org.uk. UK users may contact us about OSA matters at uk-safety@streamvault.example.
