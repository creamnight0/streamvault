Content Guidelines

Last updated: September 5, 2026

These guidelines define what may and may not be published on StreamVault. They apply to videos, live streams, thumbnails, titles, descriptions, comments, and channel art.

1. Not Allowed

  • Illegal content of any kind (see Illegal Content Reporting Policy)
  • Any content that endangers or sexualizes minors (see Child Safety Policy) — zero tolerance
  • Non-consensual recordings of identifiable people in private situations
  • Sexually explicit content — StreamVault is a general-audience platform
  • Gratuitous real-world violence, gore, or animal abuse
  • Hate speech, harassment, doxxing, or credible threats
  • Dangerous acts likely to cause serious harm if imitated, without professional context
  • Medical, financial, or civic misinformation causing real-world harm
  • Spam, scams, deceptive metadata, and artificial engagement

2. Age-Restricted (18+ label required)

  • Strong profanity throughout, mature themes, moderate violence in fictional/news context
  • Alcohol, tobacco, and gambling-focused content
  • Graphic educational or documentary material presented with context

Age-restricted videos are excluded from the homepage, ads, and viewers who have not passed age assurance (policy).

3. Monetization Standards

Advertiser-friendly content must additionally avoid recurring strong profanity, shocking imagery, and controversial-issue focus without context. Videos can be published but demonetized.

4. Enforcement Ladder

  • Warning — first minor violation, educational notice
  • Strike — content removed; 3 strikes in 12 months = channel termination
  • Immediate termination — severe violations (illegal content, child safety, repeated hate)

All decisions are appealable via the Complaints & Appeals Policy.
