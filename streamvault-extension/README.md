# StreamVault Video Clipper — Chrome Extension

A 1-Click Chrome Extension to capture video streams from any tube site (like PimpBunny) and save them permanently into your StreamVault studio.

## How to Install in Google Chrome (30 Seconds):

1. Open Google Chrome and go to: `chrome://extensions`
2. Toggle on **"Developer mode"** in the top-right corner.
3. Click **"Load unpacked"** in the top-left.
4. Select the folder:
   `/Users/imran/stream media/streamvault-extension`

## How to Use:
1. Go to any video on PimpBunny (or any video page) and hit play for a second.
2. Click the **StreamVault Clipper** icon in your Chrome toolbar.
3. It will automatically detect the direct video stream, title, and cover image.
4. Click **"⚡ Ingest & Save to StreamVault"**.
5. StreamVault's backend will download the full master video before the token expires, transcode it, and publish it permanently to your StreamVault catalog.
