Content Removal Request

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Request removal of content that violates the law or StreamVault policies.

Who Can Use This Form

Anyone — you do not need a StreamVault account. Use this form to request removal of a video, comment, or channel that violates the law or our policies, including content depicting you without consent.

Emergencies: if someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first. Child safety reports are prioritized 24/7 — see the Child Safety Policy.
Submitting knowingly false reports may lead to action against you. Reports made in good faith are always safe to file.

What Happens After You Submit

  • You receive an acknowledgment email with a case number
  • Requests are triaged by severity; child-safety and imminent-harm reports are handled first, typically within hours
  • Non-consensual intimate imagery reports are resolved within 48 hours
  • Other reports are typically resolved within 7 days
  • You will be notified of the outcome and can appeal under the Complaints & Appeals Policy

Copyright Claims

Copyright removals follow a distinct statutory process — use the DMCA procedure instead of this form.
