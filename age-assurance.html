Age Assurance Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

This policy explains how StreamVault verifies age for age-restricted features and content, and how we protect the data involved.

1. Why We Check Age

Some content and features (e.g., mature-rated videos, live chat, purchases) are limited to users above certain ages. Laws in several jurisdictions — including the UK Online Safety Act — require highly effective age assurance rather than simple self-declaration.

2. Methods We Use

  • Date of birth at signup for baseline age gating
  • Facial age estimation via an accredited third-party provider — images are processed transiently and never stored
  • ID document verification through a certified verification partner, as an alternative option
  • Digital identity / reusable age credentials where available in your region

You can choose among the methods available in your region; no single method is mandatory where alternatives exist.

3. Data Protection

  • Age assurance is performed by specialist providers under strict contracts; StreamVault receives only a yes/no age signal, never your documents or biometric data
  • Verification data is deleted by the provider after the check completes, subject to legal retention minimums
  • Age signals are stored as a simple over/under flag against your account

4. If a Check Fails

You may retry with another method or contact support. Disputes about incorrect age determinations are handled under the Complaints & Appeals Policy.
