Child Safety Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

StreamVault has a zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and any content that endangers, exploits, or sexualizes minors.

1. Prohibited Absolutely

  • Child sexual abuse material of any kind, real or simulated, including AI-generated imagery
  • Content that sexualizes minors in any way, including through captions, comments, or context
  • Grooming, solicitation, or predatory behavior toward minors
  • Content depicting minors in dangerous or abusive situations
  • Sharing personal information of minors

2. Detection & Enforcement

  • All uploads are scanned against industry hash databases (e.g., PhotoDNA-class technologies) before publication
  • Detected or reported CSAM is removed immediately, the account is terminated, and evidence is preserved
  • We report CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and cooperate with law enforcement worldwide (see Law Enforcement Requests)

3. How to Report

Use the Report button on any video, or the Illegal Content Reporting channel, or email childsafety@streamvault.example. Reports in this category are triaged ahead of all other queues, 24/7.

You can also report directly to NCMEC at report.cybertip.org or to your national hotline via INHOPE.

4. Protecting Young Users

Accounts require a minimum age of 13. Age-restricted content is gated per our Age Assurance Policy. Minors' accounts have restricted messaging and comment defaults.
